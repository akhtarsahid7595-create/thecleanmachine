PROTECTION THAT STARTS WITH PREPARATION

A better coating result begins with clean paintwork

Ceramic coating is not a substitute for preparation. The paintwork should be washed, decontaminated and assessed before the coating is applied. Where needed, machine polishing or paint correction can be discussed as part of the preparation.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Ceramic coating questions from Glasgow drivers

How much does ceramic coating cost in Glasgow?

The price depends on vehicle size, paint condition, preparation and the selected coating. Send photographs and vehicle details for a tailored quote.

Does ceramic coating prevent scratches?

Ceramic coating can improve resistance to contamination and make maintenance easier, but it is not scratch-proof or a replacement for careful washing.

Can ceramic coating be applied to any vehicle?

Most vehicles can be assessed, but the existing paint condition and previous repairs affect the preparation and expected result.
