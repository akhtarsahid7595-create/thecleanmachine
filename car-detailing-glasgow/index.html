What is the difference between valeting and detailing?

A valet is usually a practical clean, while detailing involves a more thorough and condition-led process. The Clean Machine can recommend the most suitable option for your vehicle.

Do you provide mobile car detailing?

Yes. The service is mobile across Glasgow and surrounding areas, subject to access and appointment requirements.

Can detailing remove every scratch?

No. Results depend on the depth and type of defect. The vehicle should be assessed before any promise is made about paint correction.
